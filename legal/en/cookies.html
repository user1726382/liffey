Cookies and tracking

This page describes the current website's use of cookies and tracking technologies. It will be updated if integrations change.

Current website

The website does not currently include advertising pixels or a general-purpose analytics platform. Its contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile for bot prevention. Turnstile performs browser-side verification and issues a short-lived verification token; if optional pre-clearance is enabled, Cloudflare may also issue a cf_clearance security cookie. Whether pre-clearance or other provider-side cookies are active must be verified against the deployed configuration and browser tests. Cloudflare, the website host and other infrastructure providers may process technical connection and security data independently. The website does not currently provide an advertising-cookie consent interface; the need for consent or an exemption for each actual technology must be assessed under German law.

Future changes

Before adding analytics, embedded media, advertising pixels or nonessential storage, perform a GDPR and ePrivacy/TDDDG assessment, publish a complete inventory and obtain prior consent where legally required. Withdrawal must be as easy as consent. Do not display a cosmetic cookie banner when there is nothing to consent to.
