4. Forms, applicants and analytics

This website provides a contact form for business enquiries, but does not provide direct CV uploads or newsletter subscriptions. The form collects name, email address, enquiry topic, message, and confirmation that the privacy notice has been read. Cloudflare Turnstile processes verification information to protect against automated abuse; a Cloudflare Worker validates the submission and transmits it to Resend for delivery to the relevant Liffey Consulting business email address. An acknowledgement may be sent to the email address supplied. The website asks visitors not to send CVs or sensitive information through this form. The enquiry is processed to respond to requests and manage business correspondence; the applicable lawful basis must be documented for each enquiry category (including Art. 6(1)(b) where pre-contractual steps are requested by the data subject, or Art. 6(1)(f) where justified by legitimate interests). Visitors can express interest in career profiles using their own email application and may voluntarily attach a CV. Such emails and attachments are received by the email provider rather than stored by the static website. General business enquiries can be sent by email to info@liffey-consulting.com. Email enquiries involve processing the sender address, message and any voluntarily supplied details for correspondence; the lawful basis must be confirmed for the actual operator (typically Art. 6(1)(f) for B2B enquiries, subject to balancing). Email service-provider processing terms and technical deletion settings remain to be verified. Do not send sensitive information. Before sending a CV, review the separate candidate privacy notice and contact us if additional details are required. The page code does not currently load analytics, advertising trackers or nonessential cookies. Following external links may involve processing by those sites.

6. Retention

Routine business enquiries received through the contact form or by email are normally retained for up to 12 months after the last substantive contact or closure, and deleted sooner when no longer needed. Recruitment application records for unsuccessful German candidates are normally deleted four months after the final decision; earlier deletion may apply after withdrawal. Separate rules apply where a documented legal claim or statutory duty requires retention. A candidate talent pool, if introduced, would require a separate lawful basis and a 12-month review/renewal period; this website does not currently provide talent-pool registration. Qualifying commercial correspondence may be retained for six years, accounting vouchers and invoices for eight years, and applicable accounting books and annual accounts for ten years under German law, calculated from the relevant year-end. Liffey-controlled Worker operational logs have a 30-day retention target, but the actual retention periods of independent hosting, email and anti-abuse providers depend on their verified service settings. Contact form messages are transmitted through Cloudflare and Resend to business email accounts; the static site itself does not maintain a separate enquiry database. These periods are subject to necessary legal holds, lawful exceptions and implementation of the deletion procedures.
