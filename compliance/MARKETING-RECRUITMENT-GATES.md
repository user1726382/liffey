# Recruitment, outreach and AI operational release rules
Status: DRAFT | Date: 2026-11-11

## Recruitment
1. Define whether Liffey introduces candidates, performs executive search, employs agency workers or supplies labour. No implied permission for temporary staffing.
2. For Irish activities, obtain written WRC/licensing counsel determination and licence if legally required; assess any German AÜG/AVGS obligations separately.
3. Record candidate sourcing source and collection date; provide GDPR Art. 13 or 14 information as appropriate, with exceptions individually assessed.
4. Document lawful basis, sharing recipient and purpose before disclosing candidate profiles to clients. Apply data minimisation and role-based access.
5. Assess equality/anti-discrimination and necessity of references, background checks and special-category data.
6. Define retention and deletion triggers, candidate rights workflow and secure CV handling.

## Electronic marketing
Before sending outreach, identify the jurisdiction of recipient and applicable German UWG §7 / Irish ePrivacy rules. Record channel, recipient type, consent or specific statutory exception, source, notice and suppression decision. B2B status alone is not a blanket exemption. Honour objections and opt-outs.

## AI
Do not deploy candidate scoring, ranking, filtering or automated rejection until system inventory, EU AI Act role/classification, applicable dates, transparency, human oversight and GDPR Art. 22/DPIA assessment are signed off. Administrative writing assistance must still be checked for confidentiality and vendor processing.
