# B2B services agreement — negotiation checklist and clause skeleton
Version 0.1 | Status: COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED; not an executable contract

**Parties:** Liffey GbR (full partners/address TBD) and identified business customer.
**SOW:** Describe service type, deliverables, assumptions, milestones, acceptance and exclusions. Do not promise a regulated professional service without authorisation.
**Fees:** Currency, VAT treatment subject to tax determination, invoicing schedule, payment terms, expenses and late-payment rules.
**Responsibilities:** Customer supplies lawful instructions and materials; each party has a named contact and change-control process.
**Confidentiality:** Protect non-public commercial and candidate information, permitted disclosure, exceptions, return/deletion and survival.
**Data protection:** Allocate controller/processor/joint-controller roles per actual activity. Execute GDPR Art. 28 terms where Liffey processes on customer instructions; specify recruitment disclosures and independent-controller duties where appropriate.
**Intellectual property:** Define ownership of pre-existing tools, newly created content, third-party licences, portfolio rights and use of AI-generated materials.
**Marketing:** Customer approvals, claims substantiation, copyright/music/image rights, platform rules, electronic outreach permissions and suppression responsibility.
**Recruitment:** No candidate fees without legal review; define placement trigger, refund/replacement terms, equal-treatment obligations, candidate information and licensing scope before use.
**Liability:** Obtain German and Irish legal review of limitations, exclusions and mandatory-law carveouts. Do not insert an arbitrary blanket liability waiver, particularly for intentional conduct or gross negligence.
**Termination/disputes:** Notice, payment on termination, handover, governing law, jurisdiction and mandatory-law exceptions.
**Execution gate:** No signature until entity details, tax treatment, service model and legal review are completed.
