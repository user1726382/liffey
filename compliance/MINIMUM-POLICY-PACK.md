# Minimum viable compliance document pack — drafting inventory
Status: DRAFT / implementation required. A listed policy is not proof of a functioning control.

| Document | Trigger | Minimum contents | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| GbR agreement | Now | partners, representation, contributions, profits, liability, exits | both partners + German counsel |
| Public Impressum (DE/EN) | Live commercial website | exact legal operator, partners, service address, email/contact, applicable register and VAT details only if real | both partners + German counsel |
| Website privacy notice | Website visitor processing | controller, purposes/bases, hosting/vendors, transfers, retention, rights, complaints, cookies, contact forms | privacy lead |
| Processing register (Art. 30 assessment) | Personal data operations | activities, categories, recipients, retention, security, transfer information | privacy lead |
| Supplier register and Art. 28 DPAs | Processors | supplier purpose, region, agreement, subprocessors, transfers, security | privacy lead |
| Security/incident/rights procedure | Personal data operations | access/MFA, encryption, backups, 72-hour breach assessment, rights handling, incident log | technical/privacy owners |
| Retention schedule | Personal data operations | category, purpose, legal basis, trigger, retention, deletion owner | privacy lead |
| B2B consulting MSA/SOW | Before engagement | scope, fees, VAT, liability, confidentiality, IP, deliverables, governing law | counsel |
| Marketing agreement and outreach procedure | Before marketing delivery | IP licences, approvals, lawful electronic outreach, unsubscribe, client roles | counsel |
| Recruitment agreement + candidate notice | Before recruitment | placement model, employer fees, candidate sharing, sourcing notice, equality, deletion, references | IE/DE counsel |
| AI screening assessment | If AI screening used | AI Act classification, provider/deployer role, human oversight, GDPR DPIA/Art. 22 review | counsel |
| Employment/contractor pack | If workers engaged | status, employment/payroll, confidentiality, security, DPA if needed | counsel/tax adviser |

## Publication rule
Do not generate or publish a filled Impressum or privacy notice until partner names, address, actual infrastructure and data processing are verified. Do not substitute generic legal text for a factual processing inventory.
