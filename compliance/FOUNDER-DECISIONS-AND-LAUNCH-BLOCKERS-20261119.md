## Hard blocks / conditional safeguards

2. **German cold B2B email:** §7 UWG generally requires prior express consent for advertising email even to businesses, with narrowly defined existing-customer exception. No bulk cold-email campaign or automated outreach until compliant legal basis and channel are documented. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html
3. **AI screening/ranking:** Employment/recruitment AI is an Annex III high-risk category under EU AI Act, with applicability depending on the precise system and transitional law; before use complete vendor/system classification, bias and human oversight assessment, GDPR DPIA screening, notices, access controls and qualified legal review. Do not activate candidate AI scoring now. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng
4. **Candidate upload and talent pool:** Founder preference to enable immediately does not establish GDPR readiness. Remain disabled until lawful notices, access controls, SharePoint storage flow, vendor DPAs, deletion and security tests are evidenced.
5. **Contact form:** Prepare and test but do not enable without live legal/privacy disclosures, verified processing flow, consent/notice handling and end-to-end tests.
6. **Business formation:** Execute partnership agreement, registration, tax/accounting and cross-border VAT assessment before commercial contracting/invoicing.
7. **Pricing:** Market average minus 20% is a pricing *research request*, not an approved price. Use proposal placeholders until sourced market benchmarking is recorded.
