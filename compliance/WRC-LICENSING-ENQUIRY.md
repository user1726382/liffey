# Draft enquiry — Irish Workplace Relations Commission
**To:** licensing@workplacerelations.ie
**Status:** DRAFT — DO NOT SEND UNTIL BUSINESS MODEL IS CONFIRMED

Subject: Employment Agency Act 1971 — cross-border German partnership seeking licensing guidance

Dear Licensing Section,

We are preparing a two-partner German partnership (GbR), trading as Liffey Consulting, to offer business-to-business services involving Germany and Ireland. We have not commenced recruitment activity. We are assessing whether to provide (a) permanent candidate introductions and placements for Irish employers, (b) sourcing from Germany without an Irish office or employees, and/or (c) temporary agency staffing. We have not selected a final operating model.

Could you please clarify:
1. Would a German-established agency with no Irish premises, employees or establishment require an Irish Employment Agency Act 1971 licence when sourcing or introducing candidates to employers in Ireland?
2. Does the answer change if one partner periodically works from Ireland, interviews candidates there, negotiates with Irish employers or establishes a home office?
3. Does the answer differ for candidate sourcing only, permanent placement, executive search, temporary agency staffing or employer-of-record arrangements?
4. Would a German GbR be eligible to apply directly, and what CRO business-name registration, premises, vetting and documentation would be required?
5. Can any cross-border EU services exception or notification procedure apply, and what legislation governs it?
6. Are there candidate-fee restrictions or additional reporting obligations applicable to our proposed model?
7. Please identify the authoritative statutory provisions or guidance supporting your determination.

We will not begin any activity for which an Irish licence is required until it has been granted.
