# Security and vulnerability disclosure

## Scope
The public website is hosted separately from the experimental recruitment prototype. Do not send CVs, passwords, tokens, or personal data through public GitHub issues. The prototype is not approved for candidate-data processing.

## Private reporting
The business owner reports that the dedicated mailbox `security@liffey-consulting.com` has been created. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to [security@liffey-consulting.com](mailto:security@liffey-consulting.com). Include affected URL or component, a concise description, reproduction steps that do not expose third-party data, and a safe contact method. Do not include credentials, personal data, or exploit payloads in the initial message.

**Pre-publication verification required:** confirm receipt of an email sent from outside Microsoft 365, ensure at least one named owner monitors the mailbox, and verify that a reply can be sent. No response-time commitment is made until an owner approves one.

## Handling
Do not test beyond authorization, access other people's data, disrupt services, or disclose sensitive findings publicly. The team should triage reports privately, preserve evidence, prioritize containment and remediation, and coordinate disclosure with the reporter. Production prerequisites remain in `compliance/SECURITY-RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md`.
