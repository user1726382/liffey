# Security and vulnerability disclosure

## Scope
The public website is hosted separately from the experimental recruitment prototype. Do not send CVs, passwords, tokens, or personal data through public GitHub issues. The prototype is not approved for candidate-data processing.

## Private reporting
A dedicated `security@liffey-consulting.com` mailbox is **proposed but not yet verified**. Until a monitored private reporting channel has been confirmed, do not publish vulnerabilities or exploit details in public issues. Use an existing verified private business contact route to request a secure reporting channel, without disclosing exploit details in that initial request.

Once the mailbox is provisioned, monitored and tested, replace this provisional notice with the verified address and acknowledge receipt targets. Do not claim a response SLA before the responsible owner agrees.

## Handling
Do not test beyond authorization, access other people's data, disrupt services, or disclose sensitive findings publicly. The team should triage reports privately, preserve evidence, prioritize containment and remediation, and coordinate disclosure with the reporter. Production prerequisites remain in `compliance/SECURITY-RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md`.
