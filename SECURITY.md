# Security and vulnerability disclosure

## Scope
The public website is hosted separately from the experimental recruitment prototype. Do not send CVs, passwords, tokens, or personal data through public GitHub issues. The prototype is not approved for candidate-data processing.

## Private reporting
The dedicated mailbox `security@liffey-consulting.com` has been created; the owner confirmed successful delivery of an external test message on 19 November 2026. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to [security@liffey-consulting.com](mailto:security@liffey-consulting.com). Include affected URL or component, a concise description, reproduction steps that do not expose third-party data, and a safe contact method. Do not include credentials, personal data, or exploit payloads in the initial message.

**Remaining operational checks:** assign a named person to monitor this mailbox, confirm that a reply can be sent from it, and test the escalation path. External inbound delivery has been confirmed by the owner; outgoing replies and monitoring coverage are not yet independently verified. No response-time commitment is made until an owner approves one.

## Handling
Do not test beyond authorization, access other people's data, disrupt services, or disclose sensitive findings publicly. The team should triage reports privately, preserve evidence, prioritize containment and remediation, and coordinate disclosure with the reporter. Production prerequisites remain in `compliance/SECURITY-RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md`.
